## Official requirement snapshots and War Room editorial rationale
- **Backfire** — Requirement: As the United Kingdom complete the Special Project for Medium Range Ballistic Missiles, have a rocket site, and be at war with Germany. Rationale: The United Kingdom must complete missile research, construct launch infrastructure and maintain a war against Germany simultaneously.
- **Cradle of Civilization** — Requirement: As Iraq, build the maximum infrastructure level in all core states and achieve 100% stability. Rationale: Iraq must maintain full stability and max out infrastructure across every core, a deliberate construction and political-management challenge.
- **It's Just Good Business** — Requirement: As the East India Company, own Suez and Panama. Rationale: The East India Company must secure both global canal states, requiring long-distance maritime access and a specific political route.
- **Oil Sheiks** — Requirement: As Iraq, have 500 oil production. Rationale: A very high oil output requires Iraq to expand or intensively develop petroleum production and its supporting technology.
- **ThaiTanic** — Requirement: As any nation, sink Siam's pride of the fleet.. Rationale: The targeted Siamese pride-of-the-fleet vessel must exist and be sunk in combat, making naval positioning and target availability central.
- **First train home** — Requirement: As Czechoslovakia, own any state of the Trans-Siberian Railway.. Rationale: Czechoslovakia must acquire an eastern railway state far beyond its home region, requiring major eastward territorial expansion.
- **Czechoslovaustrohungarian Union** — Requirement: As communist Czechoslovakia, complete "Expand the Union" and have Austria and Hungary in that union.. Rationale: Requires communist Czechoslovakia to complete the union focus and align both Austrian and Hungarian membership.
- **Paradisus Paradoxum** — Requirement: As Japan, proclaim the creation of a Pure Buddhist Land.. Rationale: Japan must follow the alternate Buddhist political path and achieve the territorial and focus conditions needed to proclaim a Pure Buddhist Land.
