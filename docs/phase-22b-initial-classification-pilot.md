**Caution:** The Time is on our side sample demonstrates a weakness of a simple median: an achievement with very long playtime can be classified Easy if the other dimensions are low. These pilot scores are examples for discussion rather than approved values. Determine whether time investment should impose a minimum difficulty or whether players should see separate dimension badges.
