## Official requirement snapshots and War Room editorial rationale
- **Brentry** — Requirement: As Austria, Belgium or the Netherlands be the leader of the EU and have the United Kingdom join your faction.. Rationale: A qualifying state must attain EU faction leadership and recruit the United Kingdom, demanding an unusual political path and strategic diplomacy.
- **The Magic School Bus** — Requirement: With the Per Albin Bus, drive to Cape Town.. Rationale: Sweden must acquire the special Per Albin Bus and secure a viable land movement route to distant Cape Town.
- **Red Hot Chile Peppers** — Requirement: As Communist Chile, control California. Rationale: Communist Chile must expand naval reach and overcome the United States to occupy California.
- **The Dragon Rises** — Requirement: As "Welsh Argentina", control Wales. Rationale: The Welsh Argentina political route must be established before mounting a transatlantic invasion to take Wales.
- **King of What?** — Requirement: Have Jaque Antoine Bernard as King of Chile and control Paris. Rationale: Chile must establish a specific monarch and capture distant Paris, requiring political preparation and naval projection.
- **Chilean Empire** — Requirement: Control all mainland states that border the pacific in South America, North America and Asia. Rationale: The Chilean campaign must control an enormous Pacific coastline across three continents, demanding global force projection.
- **America Decolonized!** — Requirement: As Mapuche Chile liberate all native people in North and South America. Rationale: Mapuche Chile must secure liberation of indigenous peoples across both American continents against numerous states.
- **Tsar Bomba** — Requirement: As Tsarist Russia complete the Special Project for thermonuclear bombs.. Rationale: Requires restoration of Tsarist Russia followed by costly long-term thermonuclear research and special-project completion.
