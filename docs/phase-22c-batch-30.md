## Objective and review notes
- **True Condor Legion** — Requirement: As CHL, BOL, PRU, ECU, VEN, COL - sent volunteers to Germany. Editorial rationale: A qualifying South American country must arrange war conditions and foreign-policy eligibility to dispatch volunteers to Germany.
- **Naval Arms Race** — Requirement: As Germany led by an Admiral capitulate the United Kingdom.. Editorial rationale: Germany must adopt an admiral as leader and force British capitulation, requiring substantial naval or airborne access to the British Isles.
- **Woman in a High Castle** — Requirement: As Germany with a female leader either annex the USA, including Alaska and Hawaii or have an American puppet that owns all the American starting states, including Alaska and Hawaii.. Editorial rationale: A German female-leadership route must coincide with complete US annexation or puppeting, including remote Alaska and Hawaii.
- **Lamento Boliviano** — Requirement: As Argentina, own all of Latin America except Bolivia. Editorial rationale: Argentina must acquire nearly all Latin American territories while excluding Bolivia, demanding lengthy continental campaigns and precise peace outcomes.
- **Reconquistadors** — Requirement: As Argentina or Chile, have a Conquistadors national spirit active while holding all Spanish cores. Editorial rationale: The selected South American nation must acquire a special national spirit and take every Spanish core via a transatlantic military campaign.
- **Hispanics of the world, unite!** — Requirement: As Chile, form the Hispanic Alliance and have at least 8 "Hispanic" nations in your faction. Editorial rationale: Chile must establish the Hispanic Alliance and recruit eight eligible Spanish-speaking nations, requiring substantial diplomatic coordination. Note: Steam prints 'Hispanics of the world unite!' (no comma). Preserve canonical War Room title mapping.
- **Edelweiss Seedelweiss** — Requirement: As Austria have a coastline, at least 10 Battleships and have Georg von Trapp as an Admiral. Editorial rationale: Landlocked Austria must gain coastal access, field ten battleships and unlock Georg von Trapp as an admiral, demanding huge naval-industrial buildup.
- **Otto-man** — Requirement: As Austria or Hungary, led by Otto von Habsburg, control the starting states of Austria, Hungary, Czechoslovakia, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece and Turkey as well as the states in Polish Galicia, Slovenia and Croatia.. Editorial rationale: The Otto von Habsburg restoration route must expand into nearly all of Central Europe and much of the Balkans and Anatolia, requiring multiple regional campaigns.
