## Source-backed requirements and editorial rationale
- **The Winner takes it all** — Requirement: As PRC owns and controls all starting Chinese core states.. Editorial rationale: The People's Republic of China must consolidate the complete initial Chinese core territory and hold it directly, requiring broad regional conquest.
- **Master Infiltrator** — Requirement: As PRC, don't expand beyond Yan'an, and infiltrate at least 5 Chinese states and flip them to your control when attacking the rest of China.. Editorial rationale: The PRC must avoid early expansion beyond Yan'an while preparing five successful infiltrations, then use a coordinated attack to flip the states.
- **Kuťáci** — Requirement: As Czechoslovakia, build every facility type.. Editorial rationale: Czechoslovakia must build every qualifying facility category, which takes sustained construction, technology and protected industrial development.
- **Battle Royale** — Requirement: As Communist China, fight both China and Japan before 1940 within 90 days of being at war with the other, and capitulate them both.. Editorial rationale: Communist China must enter wars against China and Japan within a ninety-day interval, before the 1940 deadline, and defeat both larger opponents.
- **It's 1812 All Over Again** — Requirement: As Canada, take and hold Washington D.C.. Editorial rationale: Canada must mount a successful campaign against the United States and retain its capital, overcoming a substantially stronger industrial adversary.
- **House of Kurds** — Requirement: As Kurdistan, hold all of your cores.. Editorial rationale: A Kurdish state must be established and unite all Kurdish core states divided among multiple regional powers.
- **A Great Hunger** — Requirement: As Hungary proclaim Greater Hungary and control all of your historical state. Editorial rationale: Hungary needs the Greater Hungary path and control of its historical territories, requiring coordinated Central European expansion.
- **Danzig queen** — Requirement: As Sweden, own and control Danzig. Editorial rationale: Sweden must acquire and hold Danzig on the Baltic coast, requiring an attainable but politically delicate Baltic military or peace-settlement objective.
