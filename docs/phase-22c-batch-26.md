## Source notes
- **Media Mughal** — Official requirement: As the Mughal Empire, complete Agency Upgrade Psychological Warfare, become Spy Master and have at least 8 operatives. Editorial reasoning: Mughal restoration must be combined with a sufficiently strong intelligence agency, spymaster standing and eight operatives.
- **Oh, how the turntables** — Official requirement: As India puppet the UK with Churchill as it's leader. Editorial reasoning: India must subjugate the United Kingdom while Churchill remains in power; overseas invasion and leader timing make the objective challenging.
- **I'm just passing by** — Official requirement: Own a state on each continent while being at peace. Editorial reasoning: A worldwide collection of owned states requires overseas access across all continents followed by a peaceful end-state.
- **That's SIkh!** — Official requirement: As Khalistan, control all of the starting Raj states. Editorial reasoning: Khalistan must emerge and then secure every territory held by the Raj at the start, requiring significant expansion across the subcontinent.
- **I shall either find a way or make one** — Official requirement: Control Rome and have an elephant unit in the state.. Editorial reasoning: Capturing Rome must be coordinated with fielding and moving an elephant unit into the state, demanding access to the special unit type.
- **Bad Ending - The whole world is now Brazil** — Official requirement: Control every state in the world. Editorial reasoning: A world conquest must gain ownership or control across every state on the map, requiring extreme military, naval and logistical effort.
- **The Merry Band** — Official requirement: As communist brazil ruled by Lampião, occupy Nottingham. Editorial reasoning: Brazil needs a specific communist leadership path and a successful invasion of Britain to occupy Nottingham.
- **I'm playing both sides, so I always come out on top** — Official requirement: Sell weapons to both sides of a civil war.. Editorial reasoning: The player must create or find a civil-war market where arms sales can be made to both opposing sides; eligibility and timing affect the outcome.
