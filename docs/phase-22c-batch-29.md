## Objective and review notes
- **Proactive Defense** — Requirement: As any communist South or Central American country, occupy Washington D.C. Editorial rationale: A communist Latin American country must project enough military power to seize the US capital despite the United States' larger economy.
- **No Friends, Only Family** — Requirement: As Austria or Hungary led by a Habsburg Monarch, control the Swiss Plateau and have at least three other countries be led by a member of the Habsburg family.. Editorial rationale: Requires Habsburg leadership in four countries and control of Swiss Plateau, combining highly specific dynastic paths with expansion.
- **Bullseye** — Requirement: Successfully launch a raid on a Landmark. Editorial rationale: A viable landmark raid needs suitable targets, the necessary capabilities and successful execution, with much less setup than a major conquest.
- **This time for Africa** — Requirement: As Ethiopia, found the African Union and have it encompass at least 13 different countries with capitals in Africa.. Editorial rationale: Ethiopia must survive its vulnerable start, establish the African Union and arrange membership of thirteen African-capital countries.
- **Revenge for 1925** — Requirement: As Iraq, form the Hashemite Arab Federation and defeat Saudi Arabia. Editorial rationale: Iraq must unlock and form a specific federation and then secure a decisive victory over Saudi Arabia through regional expansion.
- **Cheese your fighter.** — Requirement: As Norway, with Peder Jarlsberg as country leader, own Telemark, Glouchestershire, Normandy, Lombardia and Holland.. Editorial rationale: A specified Norwegian leader must remain in power while Norway owns a scattered set of distant European states, requiring multiple overseas campaigns.
- **Genghis Khan't** — Requirement: As any Chinese country or Manchukuo, own and control all Mongolian core states.. Editorial rationale: A Chinese state or Manchukuo must secure Mongolia's core territories, requiring northern expansion and ownership plus control of all objectives.
- **Revenge for the triple alliance** — Requirement: As Paraguay, declare war on Argentina, Brazil and Uruguay within the same month. Editorial rationale: Small Paraguay must arrange simultaneous hostilities against its three larger neighbors within one calendar month, an unusually risky timing challenge.
