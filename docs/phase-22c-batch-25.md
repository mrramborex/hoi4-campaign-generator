## Source notes
- **Avenging the Sack of Baghdad** — Official requirement: As Iraq, control the capital of Mongolia. Editorial reasoning: Iraq must establish an overland campaign through Asia and seize Mongolia's capital; strategic access through large neighbors is difficult.
- **Et tu, Paulus?** — Official requirement: Capture a field Marshal.. Editorial reasoning: Requires combat conditions that yield the capture of an enemy field marshal rather than ordinary division destruction; timing and opposing commanders add uncertainty.
- **Hunt for the Desert Fox** — Official requirement: Capture Erwin Rommel.. Editorial reasoning: The opposing army must field Rommel and the player must capture him in battle; availability of the named commander makes the opportunity unpredictable.
- **This achievement is dedicated to the brave soldiers of...** — Official requirement: As Afghanistan, capitulate the Soviet Union. Editorial reasoning: Afghanistan must expand from a weak industrial and military base and force Soviet capitulation across a huge theater.
- **I have the high ground** — Official requirement: As Afghanistan, control all Himalyan states. Editorial reasoning: Afghanistan must seize high-altitude Himalayan territories across several countries; mountain logistics and strong neighboring armies complicate expansion.
- **The Sands of Time** — Official requirement: As Iran, own the entire Middle East. Editorial reasoning: Iran must conquer or acquire a large number of Middle Eastern states and handle both regional powers and overseas-held territory.
- **The Legacy of Cyrus the Great** — Official requirement: As Iran, become the owner of former Achaemenid Empire's states. Editorial reasoning: The Achaemenid territorial footprint extends well beyond modern Iran into multiple strategic regions and powerful neighbors.
- **From Tehran with Love** — Official requirement: As Iran, guarantee Poland's independence and ensure it does not capitulate by 1945.. Editorial reasoning: Iran must secure a guarantee and protect Poland through the 1945 cutoff, requiring a difficult intervention in European war conditions.
