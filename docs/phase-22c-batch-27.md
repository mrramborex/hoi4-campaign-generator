## Source-backed requirements and editorial rationale
- **Savior of the holy lands** — Requirement: As Afghanistan, become the owner of Palestine and Mecca. Editorial rationale: Afghanistan must gain both Palestine and Mecca despite limited opening resources, requiring expansion through heavily contested Middle Eastern territories.
- **Its all coming together** — Requirement: As China, fully control all core states alone or with subjects.. Editorial rationale: China must consolidate all of its core territory either directly or through subjects, requiring the defeat or integration of rival claimants and possible occupiers.
- **The Coast is Clear** — Requirement: As China, never lose a starting coastal state before 1945.9.2.. Editorial rationale: China must preserve every initial coastal state throughout the long Japanese threat until September 2, 1945, with zero tolerance for temporary loss.
- **A Fullmetal Brotherhood** — Requirement: As Fascist China, control 300 Steel, Tungsten and Chromium.. Editorial rationale: Fascist China needs enormous resource control in three materials, especially chromium beyond its usual resource base, and a suitable political alignment.
- **The Sun Will Never Set** — Requirement: As Japan, proclaim the Eternal Empire and control all of your core states.. Editorial rationale: Japan must complete its Eternal Empire political path and hold all resulting national cores, potentially involving territorial recovery or expansion.
- **From Rags to Admiral Stripes** — Requirement: Promote a Ship Captains to Admiral.. Editorial rationale: This is a naval officer advancement objective requiring the ability to promote a ship captain, with comparatively little territorial expansion needed.
- **Doctrine of Choice** — Requirement: Complete three subdoctrines in the same track.. Editorial rationale: The main investment is earning enough doctrine progress to complete three subdoctrines within one chosen track rather than spreading progress between branches.
- **The Neverending Story** — Requirement: As a Warlord, proclaim a new Chinese Empire.. Editorial rationale: A Chinese warlord must consolidate sufficient territory and political legitimacy to proclaim a new Chinese Empire under demanding regional-war conditions.
