Standing Orders

Campaign Rules & Operational Doctrine

FIELD
MANUALWR–17G
DOCUMENT PURPOSE

How a War Room Campaign Works

Standing Orders are the permanent rules behind every War Room assignment. They explain what the Generator gives you, what counts as completing an operation, and how that campaign is recorded. These rules are guidance—not restrictions on how you enjoy Hearts of Iron IV.

SECTION I
STANDING ORDERSSTANDARD CAMPAIGN DOCTRINE
01
COUNTRY ASSIGNMENT

Accept Your Command

The nation selected by the War Room is your assigned country for the operation.

02
POLITICAL DIRECTIVE

Follow the Assigned Route

Pursue the generated political path far enough to establish its intended government or alignment. Do not deliberately switch to an unrelated route simply to bypass the assignment.

03
PRIMARY OBJECTIVE

Complete the Assignment

Your assigned achievement is the campaign's primary objective. When no applicable achievement exists, standing orders become: Conquer the world as this country.

04
VICTORY CONDITION

Finish the Operation

Completing the assigned achievement or fallback objective qualifies the operation as completed.

05
REROLL PROTOCOL

Commander Discretion

Rerolls are permitted. The War Room exists to create interesting campaigns, not to police how you play them.

06
CAMPAIGN RECORDS

File the Operation

Once Start Campaign is pressed, the assignment enters Campaign History and may be recorded as In Progress, Completed, or Abandoned.

SECTION II
SPECIAL ORDERSOPTIONAL CAMPAIGN MODIFIERS • REFERENCE

Special Orders are optional restrictions that can be added to a generated operation for extra challenge. This manual defines them; Operation Parameters will control whether the Generator assigns them.

HISTORICAL COMMAND

Run the operation with Historical AI Focuses enabled.

NO RETREAT

Do not manually delete trapped divisions simply to avoid their destruction.

LONE WOLF

Do not join or create a faction unless faction membership is required by the primary objective.

LIMITED CONSCRIPTION

Do not raise your conscription law above Extensive Conscription.

NO COLLABORATION

Complete the operation without establishing collaboration governments.

GENERAL'S WAR

Launch offensive operations with army battle plans. Manual movement is reserved for defense, encirclement cleanup, and emergencies.

DOMESTIC ARSENAL

Do not use licensed foreign equipment production. Your war machine must rely on your own designs and production lines.

SELF-SUFFICIENT

Do not request lend-lease from another country. Fight with what your own industry and existing support can provide.

OPEN GROUND

Do not construct land or coastal forts during the operation unless the primary objective explicitly requires them.

CONVENTIONAL WARFARE

Do not use nuclear weapons during the operation unless the primary objective explicitly requires a nuclear strike.

BOOTS ON THE GROUND

Do not launch paradrop operations unless airborne forces are explicitly required by the primary objective.

HARDCORE ORDERSHIGH RISK • OPTIONAL

Hardcore Orders impose substantially harsher restrictions. They are issued only when Hardcore mode is selected in Operation Parameters.

NO SECOND CHANCES

If your nation capitulates, the operation is abandoned. Liberation or government-in-exile recovery does not save the campaign.

NATIONAL ARSENAL

No requested lend-lease and no licensed foreign equipment production. Your military must be supplied by your own production.

NO PUPPET MASTERS

Do not create or release puppets during the operation unless the primary objective requires a subject nation.

AGAINST THE CLOCK

Complete the primary objective before January 1, 1945.

BLOOD & STEEL

Do not request expeditionary forces. Your own armed forces must carry the operation.

OLD SCHOOL WARFARE

No nuclear strikes, paradrops, collaboration governments, or licensed foreign equipment production.
